# Legion TD Community Enhanced 1.1.3

**Death Knight — Mirror of Truth.** The final upgrade reveals the chosen area to your team for **20 seconds**. The reveal is applied after normal unit vision and expires when the timer ends. The spell can be used once per match.

The packaged spell passed a simulated timer check for both teams. The duration still needs confirmation in a live Warcraft III match.
