# Legion TD Community Enhanced 1.1.2

| Unit | Change |
| --- | --- |
| **Guardian** | Entangling Roots recharges in **12 seconds**, up from 6. The root still lasts 8 seconds. |
| **Wolverine** | Madness grants **1.5 seconds of damage immunity**, down from 3. |
| **Death Knight** | The final upgrade’s **Mirror of Truth** reveals the chosen area to your team for **6 seconds**. The ability can be used once per match. |

The in-game tooltips reflect the updated durations. The Mirror of Truth spell handler was checked for both teams, including the single-use limit and removal of vision after six seconds, with simulated Warcraft III engine calls. A live Warcraft III match is still needed to confirm the visual result.
