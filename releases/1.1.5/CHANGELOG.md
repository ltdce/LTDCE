# Legion TD Community Enhanced 1.1.5

This update addresses the Druid and Keeper of the Grove walking back and forth after casting or aborting a spell. When an eligible enemy is already in range, these fighters resume attacking it and queue their usual route. When no target is available, they follow their usual route immediately.

The order logic was checked in simulated lane and arena scenarios. The movement still needs confirmation in a live Warcraft III match.
